GATE CSE 2027: Digital Logic
Exam-oriented notes, formula sheet, and PYQ solutions for the Digital Logic section of GATE CSE.
About
Goal: Full marks in Digital Logic (GATE CSE 2027)
Language: Simple English, standard technical terminology
Style: Concise, structured, exam-oriented. No filler.
Reference books: Mano (primary), Kohavi, Brown and Vranesic, Malvino and Leach. See `Resources/Book_References.md`.
Official GATE Syllabus
Boolean algebra and minimization (algebraic technique, Karnaugh map, tabular method). Design of combinational and sequential circuits. Number representation and arithmetic (fixed and floating point).

Planned Chapters
01 Number Systems: Introduction and Base Conversion, Arithmetic Operations, Complements and Signed Numbers, Overflow Detection, Codes (BCD, Excess-3, Self-Complementing, Gray, Alphanumeric), Integer Representation, Floating Point and IEEE 754
02 Boolean Algebra: Introduction and Boolean Laws, Logic Gates and Universal Gates, Duality, De Morgan and Consensus Theorem, SOP/POS and Canonical Forms, K-Map (up to 5 Variables), Tabular Method (Quine-McCluskey)
03 Combinational Circuits: Introduction, Half/Full Adder, Half/Full Subtractor, BCD Adder and BCD to Excess-3 Converter, Look-Ahead Carry Adder, MUX, DEMUX and Decoder, Encoders and Priority Encoder, Comparator and Parity, ROM/PLA/PAL and Function Implementation, Hazards
04 Sequential Circuits: Introduction, Latches, Flip-Flops and Excitation Tables, Master-Slave and Race-Around, Flip-Flop Conversions, Registers, Counters, FSM (Mealy and Moore), State Minimization and Sequence Detectors, Booth's Algorithm, Memory Basics
Each Chapter Folder Will Contain
`Notes.md`: overview, definitions, formulas and tables, shortcuts, common mistakes, GATE question pattern, quick revision summary
`PYQs.md`: question (year, set), concept, step-by-step solution, final answer, shortcut, note
Workflow
Watch the lecture and log it in `Resources/Course_Lecture_Map.md`
Study the chapter notes and add formulas to `Resources/Formula_Sheet.md`
Solve PYQs and record them in `Resources/PYQ_Tracker.md`
Log every mistake in `Resources/Error_Log.md`
Revise by priority: High, then Medium, then Low
